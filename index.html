Frequently Asked Questions

Questions, answered.

Here are answers to some of the most common questions about working with Ambition IT.

Still have a question? Get in touch →
How much does a website cost?

The cost depends on the number of pages, functionality and support your business needs. You can view our pricing or use the instant quote tool to receive an initial estimate.

How do payments work?

For projects costing more than £999, 50% is paid before work begins and the remaining 50% is paid after the website has been completed and approved. Payment terms for projects of £999 or less are confirmed in your quotation.

How long does a website take to build?

The timeframe depends on the size and complexity of the website and how quickly content and feedback are provided. A clear estimated timeframe will be agreed before work begins.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Yes. Every Ambition IT website is designed to work across mobile phones, tablets, laptops and desktop computers.

Do you provide hosting and domains?

Hosting and domain options can be discussed as part of your project. These costs are shown separately so you understand which charges are one-off and which are recurring.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We can review your existing website and recommend improvements to its design, content, navigation, performance and mobile experience.

Can I update the website after launch?

This depends on how your website is built. Where appropriate, you can be given access to update content yourself. Optional website management is also available if you would prefer Ambition IT to handle updates.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Optional ongoing website management and support are available after launch, including remote assistance and on-site support where appropriate.
