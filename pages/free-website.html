Before you apply

Is the website really free?+

Yes. Our design and build time is free. You cover the domain and hosting needed to keep it online.

How is the business selected?+

We consider the potential impact, the business story and whether the project suits a focused one-page website.

What if I am not selected this month?+

With your permission, we can consider your application again in a future month.

What do you ask for in return?+

Honest feedback, permission to feature the work in our portfolio and, if you are happy, a testimonial.
